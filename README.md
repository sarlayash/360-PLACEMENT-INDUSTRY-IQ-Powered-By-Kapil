### How to Install on Any Mobile Device or Computer (100% Offline)
The application is a progressive web app (PWA) with zero cloud dependencies for assessments. After opening it once, it can be used completely offline anywhere in the world:

- **📱 Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet):**
  1. Open [the live app URL](https://sarlayash.github.io/360-PLACEMENT-INDUSTRY-IQ-Powered-By-Kapil/) in your browser.
  2. Tap the **"⬇️ Install"** button in the top navigation bar, or tap the three dots menu `(⋮)` and select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install App"**.
  3. The app is added directly to your mobile home screen and app drawer.

- **🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari):**
  1. Open the URL in Safari.
  2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Launch directly from your home screen as a standalone full-screen offline app.

- **💻 Windows, Mac & Linux (Chrome / Edge / Brave):**
  1. Open the URL in your desktop browser.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar or click **"⬇️ Install"** in the top header.
  3. The app launches in its own dedicated, native desktop window.

## 🎯 Core Product Philosophy

Conventional MCQ exams test static factual recall and rote memorization. In contrast, **360° Placement & Industry IQ** simulates:
> **"A simulated day in your professional life — 100 Real Professional Decisions."**

Each question presents a realistic, high-stakes scenario across resource trade-offs, cross-functional misalignment, ethical boundaries, customer crises, and strategic growth. All 4 choices are plausible, professional actions embodying distinct behavioral heuristics.

## ⚡ Key Architectural Features

- **100 Unique Questions Across 5 Difficulty Levels:**
  - *Level 1 — Foundation (Q1–20)*
  - *Level 2 — Professional (Q21–40)*
  - *Level 3 — Industry (Q41–60)*
  - *Level 4 — Advanced (Q61–80)*
  - *Level 5 — Executive (Q81–100)*
- **Smart Domain Advisor:** Dynamically analyzes candidate domain (Technology, Finance, Marketing, Healthcare, Operations, etc.) and recommends tailored tracks.
- **Adaptive Micro-Scenarios:** Triggers immediate situational follow-ups (stakeholder pushback, supply chain shocks) to test behavioral stability under pressure.
- **Anti-Gaming Engine:** Tracks consistency, authenticity, decision stability, risk orientation, and response confidence without punitive language.
- **Time Intelligence:** Tracks response cadence and calculates Decision Speed Index (rewarding thoughtful deliberation).
- **Interactive 20-Axis SVG Radar Chart:** Smooth vector spider chart mapping all 20 dimensions with interactive tooltip feedback.
- **Career Weather Map:** Visual indicator across all 20 domains (`🟢 Strong`, `🟡 Developing`, `🟠 Watch`, `🔴 Priority Focus`).
- **Professional DNA:** Discovers archetypes (*The Structured Problem Solver*, *The Adaptive Builder*, *The Strategic Communicator*, etc.).
- **Skill Gap Bridge:** Action ladder: `CURRENT → TARGET → ACTION → PRACTICE → REASSESS`.
- **Career Simulation:** Projects first 30 days, 90 days, and 6 months in the workplace.
- **Offline PDF Generation & QR Verification:** Generates cryptographic assessment ID (`SY-360-IQ-2026-XXXXXX`) and authentic SVG QR verification token.
- **Procedural Sound Engine:** Built via Web Audio API with zero external audio assets.
- **Attempt History & "What Changed?" Comparison:** Compare multiple attempts to analyze decision movement.
- **Data Privacy Safeguard:** 100% client-side storage with a "Delete All My Data" button.
